Privacy Policy

VM Solutions Manager Toolkit · Last updated 25 August 2026

This page explains what information the VM Solutions Manager Toolkit ("the Toolkit") collects, how it's stored, and who can access it. The Toolkit is an internal operational tool used by team managers at VM Solutions / Vergo Marketing Solutions to track team performance, generate weekly reports, and log tooling/training requests.

What information is collected

The Toolkit stores information entered directly by the manager using it. This includes:

  • Team member names and channel/role (e.g. Email Outbound, Organic Outreach, Cold Calling)
  • Performance inputs: logged days, hours worked, activity volume, output results, and quality audit scores
  • Requests and blockers raised in 1-on-1 conversations (tooling, training, process, or people requests)
  • Free-text report fields, which may include manager observations on attitude, discipline, or performance trends

The Toolkit does not collect any information automatically from team members themselves — nothing is gathered by tracking, monitoring, or observing anyone directly through this tool. All data is manually entered by the manager.

How the information is used

Data entered into the Toolkit is used solely to:

  • Calculate weekly effort and output scores for internal performance review
  • Generate the standard weekly performance report for leadership
  • Track tooling, training, and process requests raised by the team

This data is used internally within VM Solutions / Vergo Marketing Solutions only. It is not sold, rented, or shared with any external advertiser or third party for marketing purposes.

Where the information is stored

Data is stored using Supabase, a third-party database provider, and the Toolkit itself is hosted on Vercel. Both providers may process data on infrastructure located outside Australia. Neither Supabase nor Vercel has any independent access to or use of the data beyond providing the underlying storage and hosting service.

Who can access this data

Access to the Toolkit is limited to the manager(s) it was set up for, and to VM Solutions leadership as described in the Team Manager Operating Pack. The Toolkit does not currently require a login code; anyone with the direct web address can view and edit the data. If this changes, this policy will be updated to reflect the access model in place.

Data about individual employees

Because the Toolkit stores performance evaluations, attitude observations, and requests tied to named individuals, some of the information here may constitute personal information about employees under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth). Employee records held for the purposes of an existing employment relationship are generally exempt from the Australian Privacy Principles, but this exemption has limits and doesn't cover every situation. If a team member has questions about the data held about them, they should raise this with their manager or with VM Solutions leadership directly.

Data retention

Data remains stored until manually deleted by the manager or by VM Solutions leadership. There is currently no automatic deletion schedule.

Questions or concerns

Any questions about this policy, or requests relating to data held in the Toolkit, can be directed to VM Solutions leadership.

This policy describes the Toolkit's current data practices in plain language. It is not a substitute for formal legal advice. Given that this tool processes employee performance data used in decisions affecting people's employment, VM Solutions should have this policy — and its broader data-handling practices — reviewed by a qualified employment or privacy lawyer.
