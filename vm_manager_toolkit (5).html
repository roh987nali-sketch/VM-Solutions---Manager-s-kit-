Privacy Policy

VM Solutions Manager Toolkit · Last updated 25 August 2026

This page explains what information the Toolkit collects, how it's stored, and who can access it. The Toolkit is an internal operational tool used by team managers at VM Solutions / Vergo Marketing Solutions to track team performance, generate weekly reports, and log tooling/training requests.

What information is collected

The Toolkit stores information entered directly by the manager using it, including:

  • Team member names and channel/role (e.g. Email Outbound, Organic Outreach, Cold Calling)
  • Performance inputs: logged days, hours worked, activity volume, output results, and quality audit scores
  • Requests and blockers raised in 1-on-1 conversations
  • Free-text report fields, which may include manager observations on attitude, discipline, or performance trends

Nothing is collected automatically — all data is manually entered by the manager. The Toolkit does not monitor or track team members directly.

How the information is used

Data entered is used solely to calculate weekly effort/output scores, generate the standard weekly performance report for leadership, and track tooling/training/process requests. It's used internally within VM Solutions / Vergo Marketing Solutions only — not sold, rented, or shared with any external advertiser or third party for marketing purposes.

Where the information is stored

Data is stored using Supabase, a third-party database provider, and the Toolkit is hosted on Vercel. Both may process data on infrastructure outside Australia. Neither has independent access to or use of the data beyond providing storage/hosting.

Who can access this data

Access is limited to the manager(s) this Toolkit was set up for, and to VM Solutions leadership. The Toolkit does not currently require a login code — anyone with the direct web address can view and edit the data. This policy will be updated if that changes.

Data about individual employees

Because the Toolkit stores performance evaluations, attitude observations, and requests tied to named individuals, some of this information may constitute personal information about employees under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth). Employee records held for an existing employment relationship are generally exempt from the Australian Privacy Principles, but this exemption has limits. Team members with questions about data held about them should raise this with their manager or VM Solutions leadership directly.

Data retention & questions

Data remains stored until manually deleted by the manager or VM Solutions leadership — there is no automatic deletion schedule. Any questions about this policy, or requests relating to data held here, can be directed to VM Solutions leadership.

This policy describes the Toolkit's current data practices in plain language. It is not a substitute for formal legal advice. Given that this tool processes employee performance data used in decisions affecting people's employment, VM Solutions should have this policy — and its broader data-handling practices — reviewed by a qualified employment or privacy lawyer.
